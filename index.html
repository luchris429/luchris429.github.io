Chris Lu

I am a second-year DPhil student at the University of Oxford, where I am advised by Professor Jakob Foerster at FLAIR. My work focuses on applying evolution-inspired techniques to meta-learning and multi-agent reinforcement learning.

Previously, I worked as a researcher at Covariant.ai.
